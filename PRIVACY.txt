PRIVACY POLICY

App Name: Windows and Android Application Monitor Pro
Developer: SoluCore-Tech

1. DATA COLLECTION (OUR APP)
Our application is designed with extreme privacy and high performance in mind. We do NOT collect, store, transmit, or harvest any personal data, user accounts, or private information from your computer or connected Android devices. All performance data metrics (CPU, RAM, GPU, Battery, and process names) are read locally in real-time and remain entirely on your machine.

2. THIRD-PARTY ADVERTISING SDKs
To provide free access, this application loads advertisements using third-party networks (Adsterra). These third-party services may automatically collect certain technical information when rendering ads, including:
- Internet Protocol (IP) address (used for geo-targeting and delivering localized ads).
- Device identifiers, operating system version, and browser characteristics.
These third-party providers handle your data according to their own privacy policies. We do not have access to, control over, or storage of this data.

3. IN-APP PURCHASES
All premium transactions are processed securely through the Microsoft Store infrastructure. We do not collect or store financial data, credit card numbers, or billing addresses.

4. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY
We reserve the right to modify this privacy policy at any time. Any changes will be updated directly within the application repository or store listing.
